About the project

Travel Up is a web system developed for a tourism company, with the goal of facilitating both the sale of travel packages to clients and the internal management of data by the administrative team.

In practice, it's a platform where clients can create an account, browse available destinations, add trips to a cart, and complete the purchase — while administrators can manage the entire catalog and control who uses the system.

Technologies

The project was developed as a Full Stack application, with a front-end in JavaScript, HTML, and CSS, and a back-end hosted on the Render service, communicating via a REST API.
